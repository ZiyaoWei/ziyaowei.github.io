Isochords

Isochords plots the twelve notes of the chromatic scale onto the Tonnetz: a triangular grid where moving right is a perfect fifth and moving up is a major third. On this grid, every major or minor chord traces a triangle — pointing up for major, down for minor.

A triangle stays invisible until all three of its notes are sounding, then fades in. Watch a chord progression play and the triangles bloom and fade across the grid, tracing the shape of the harmony as it moves.
